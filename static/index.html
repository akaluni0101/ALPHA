Ask us anything ↗
What kind of sound can ALPHA analyze?+

ALPHA works with short recordings from rotating and cyclic equipment such as fans, pumps, valves, and slide rails. It is trained to distinguish steady operating signatures from irregular acoustic patterns.

Do I need to install a new sensor?+

No. You can upload a recording or use a browser microphone. For production deployments, ALPHA can also connect to an existing acoustic capture workflow.

What does “uncertain” mean?+

It means the signal sits between the normal and irregular decision bands. Instead of forcing a guess, ALPHA asks for a human inspection or another recording.

Where is audio processed?+

The prototype processes audio locally through the application service. Raw recordings are not persisted; only the structured analysis result is logged.

Can it identify the exact failed part?+

ALPHA currently surfaces acoustic health and irregularity. Exact component diagnosis depends on the machine type, training data, and a dedicated model.

How quickly can we start?+

You can run the first scan immediately with the demo samples on this page. A production pilot starts with a small set of normal and irregular recordings from your environment.
